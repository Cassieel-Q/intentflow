# Evidence record shape

Use the smallest records that let a future reader recheck a claim. Do not create empty `research/` folders for requirements that skip evidence work.

## `source.md`

| ID | 原始链接/路径 | 发布者或平台 | 原始日期 | 查阅日期 | 对象与范围 | 采集方式/样本 | 局限 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S1 | Direct link or local path | Source identity | Date or unknown | YYYY-MM-DD | Who/where | Known method or unknown | Selection/access limits |

Do not put conclusions in the source list. If a source cannot be reopened, state how it was preserved and avoid presenting it as independently verified.

If a search finds no usable source, record the search terms, channels, access date, and exclusion reason below the table. Write “未找到可用来源”; do not invent a source row or source ID. Record the resulting evidence gap in `research/demand-findings.md`.

## `research/demand-findings.md`

1. **研究问题与适用范围**：哪个产品决策需要证据；目前已确认与未确认的人群/场景。
2. **证据**：可核对的观察，逐条附 source ID、位置/短引文或数据定义，注明证据强弱。
3. **相反证据与偏差**：不支持初始想法的材料、渠道偏差、不可见样本和时间限制。
4. **推断**：证据支持的最窄结论及无法推出的结论。
5. **建议**：2–3 个可选方向及其取舍；标为助手建议，直到用户确认。
6. **缺口与下一步**：还需用户决策、访谈、数据或直接进入需求产出。

Never calculate a percentage without a defined numerator, denominator, and collection scope. A synthetic example must carry a visible `模拟示例，不能作为需求证据` label wherever it appears.
